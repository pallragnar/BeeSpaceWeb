Pitch

Plant flowers and deploy tech from orbit in this roguelike deckbuilder! Terraform tiny hex grid islands as advanced space Bees. Contend with fickle weather, strange creatures, mysterious artifacts and meddling bosses as you reclaim Earth for all Bee-Kind!

Description

Bee Space is a cozy, roguelike, strategic terraforming deck builder, following the adventures of a handful of bees as they embark on reclaiming the Earth for all Bee-kind.

Their honey bee ancestors were sent to space in the 80s as a science project. After a while, contact with the humans was lost, but the bees kept evolving and thriving. Now, countless generations later, the bees are returning to reclaim the little blue planet and find out what happened to its old inhabitants.

Build out your unique deck each run. Buy cards and exotic charms from the commissary shop to create amazing combos and synergies that help you throughout your expedition to reclaim Earth.

Enjoy a unique and relaxing mix of solitaire-like puzzle mechanics and strategy by playing cards from your hand to place plants and structures from orbit on a hex-grid island in order to terraform it. Plan your moves carefully to take advantage of fickle weather, deal with strange creatures, and recover mysterious human artifacts that affect the rules.

Each expedition is unique, with a campaign map traveling across three different regions. Every island you visit is different from the last, constructed from hex tiles of various types and heights that affect cards in different ways. Some islands you terraform, while others contain mysteries or inhabitants that will affect your chances of success.

Explore Earth and the bizarre human culture through the lens of whimsical sentient space bees. Discover new cards, artifacts, charms, characters and abilities to assist you on your expedition. Reclaim Earth one island at a time for the all Bee-Kind!
